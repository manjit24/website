Tradeify TV ↗ · YouTube · Setup design & build

Stream & record YouTube group shows with custom layouts

I designed and built a pro live-streaming setup for Tradeify TV's six-person YouTube show, using OBS and VDO.Ninja. It has 100+ custom scene layouts for every mix of guests and screen shares, and runs from a Stream Deck — by a producer, or solo. Whatnot + YouTube · Live producer & preset creator

Vertical + horizontal OBS streaming & recording preset

I built a custom OBS preset that streams to Whatnot (vertical) and YouTube (horizontal) at the same time, with up to four guests joining through VDO.Ninja. It records both formats plus isolated video and audio for every guest, and adds interactive memes and a soundboard. All processing, background removal included, runs on the producer's computer to keep the load off guests' machines. I also produced the show live, managing the full stream across both platforms from setup to execution.
