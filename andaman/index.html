Ferries

Fares per person · checked mid-October 2026

Private ferries run one way round the loop: Port Blair → Havelock → Neil → Port Blair. There's no regular private boat from Port Blair straight to Neil, or from Neil to Havelock. Stays

You need seven nights: four in Port Blair (24, 25, 26 and 30 October) and three on Havelock (27–29). You won't sleep on Neil. Spending the last night in Port Blair means a cancelled ferry can't make you miss the flight.

Six people can't share one hotel room. Hotels allow two adults plus one extra bed per room and check every guest's ID at check-in. Book two rooms with an extra bed each, a family suite or connecting rooms, or take a whole villa.
